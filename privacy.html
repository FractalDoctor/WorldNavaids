Privacy Policy

World Navaids
Last updated: November 2026

Data Collection

World Navaids does not collect personal data. There is no account, registration or sign-in, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us. We run no server.

Location

World Navaids uses your location only when you choose Use My Position, to measure the distance, bearing and radial from where you are to each beacon. These figures are worked out on your phone. Your position is never stored and never leaves your phone.

The widget uses your location only if you allow it, to find the nearest beacon and airport, and works it out on your phone in the same way.

If you do not allow location, you can measure from a place you search for or coordinates you type. A place search is answered by Apple’s maps service.

Data Updates

A copy of the beacon and airport data ships with the app. When you choose Update Now, the app downloads the latest public files from OurAirports. The request carries no information about you or your use of the app.

Data On Your Phone

Your routes, favourites, saved reference points and settings are stored on your phone, and are not synced or sent anywhere. All of it is removed when you delete the app.

Analytics And Tracking

World Navaids contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party tracking of any kind.

Third-Party Services

Apple supplies the maps and the place search. OurAirports publishes the data files the app downloads when you ask it to. Their handling of requests is governed by their own policies.

Children’s Privacy

World Navaids collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the World Navaids GitHub repository.
